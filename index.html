App information

Dumb Specie

A private video-preparation tool for the owner of the Dumb Specie YouTube channel.

The tool prepares The Impossible Ten puzzle episodes: video, narration, music, captions and thumbnails. It uses YouTube API Services to deliver approved videos to the owner’s channel for private review.

The channel owner checks the result and decides what to publish in YouTube Studio.

From episode to review

  1. Prepare the episode and three Shorts.
  2. Check the files and approved channel.
  3. Upload four videos privately.
  4. The owner reviews and publishes.

Who can use it?

This is an owner-operated tool. It is not open for public registration or connection of other people’s YouTube accounts. This website provides app and policy information; it does not connect to your account.

Google account access

The owner authorises access on Google’s consent screen. The tool checks the selected channel and uses the connection to upload video, attach captions and thumbnails, and verify the uploaded video’s status.

Your information and choices

Read the Privacy policy for the data used, where it is stored and how to request deletion. The Terms of service explain use of the tool and the owner’s responsibilities.
