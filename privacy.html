Privacy policy

This policy describes the Dumb Specie video-preparation tool and this information website.

Last updated: 8 October 2026

Current service status. Uploads are paused. Historical-data removal and verification remain incomplete, as explained below. The tool is not accepting new account connections.

Who operates the app

Dumb Specie is operated by the channel owner. Questions, access requests, corrections or deletion requests can be sent to imperfecttrainer@gmail.com. The tool is for the owner’s channel; this website offers no public account registration.

What the tool accesses and why

The tool uses YouTube API Services. Google handles sign-in and consent; the tool does not ask for or store a Google password.

InformationPurpose
Channel identityChannel ID, name and handle identify the authorised channel and prevent uploads to a different destination.
Connection credentialsAn authorisation token and app credentials let approved runs connect to Google without another sign-in each time.
Video and caption detailsVideo IDs, channel association, privacy status and caption-track details let the tool check uploads and avoid duplicating video or captions.
Prepared content and review recordsVideos, audio, captions, thumbnails, titles, descriptions, file fingerprints and upload results support delivery, review and recovery after a failed run.

The requested YouTube permission is youtube.force-ssl. Google describes this permission broadly, including viewing, editing and deleting videos, ratings, comments and captions. The current delivery code uses it for channel checks, private video uploads, captions and thumbnails; it has no public publishing or video-deletion function. It does not collect viewer profiles, comments, ratings or channel analytics.

Storage and access

App credentials and the refresh token are stored in GitHub Actions secrets. The owner’s original local connection file may also remain on their computer. Short-lived access tokens are used during a run. Credentials are not part of the public website or media archive.

Private GitHub storage holds original episode records and recovery archives. Current account-associated upload receipts and delivery records are kept in a separate private record store. Uploaded videos and their attached assets are stored by YouTube. The owner and people the owner authorises to administer the relevant accounts may access these private records. Access controls reduce risk but cannot make storage tamper-proof.

Sharing and permitted use

Google and YouTube receive requests and content needed to connect the account and deliver the approved videos. GitHub hosts the private processing and records. Email enquiries are handled through the support inbox. These providers may process information outside the UK under their own policies.

Google user data is used for the channel-management functions described here. It is not sold, used for advertising or profiling, or used to train general-purpose models. The app’s use and transfer of Google user data is subject to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including its Limited Use requirements. People should access private account data only with the owner’s permission or when necessary for security or legal obligations.

Retention and deletion: current implementation

Production-result download files are configured to expire after seven days; preview-media download files expire after three days. Separate workflow logs, repository history, review issues, recovery archives and the owner’s local copies have different retention settings. They do not all disappear when a download expires.

Current account records are held in a separate private, replaceable record store. Tested controls can refresh channel, video and caption facts, remove an approved current account-record copy, and stop delivery when access or records need review. Persistent guards help prevent duplicate uploads. Delivery remains paused pending a real connection check, metadata refresh and activation. Historical result downloads and affected upload logs have been removed, and private delivery links have been removed from a review issue. The state branch has been reset while preserving original episode records and guards. Older account-data objects are still retrievable from the hosting provider; removal of those retained objects and verification of local copies and backups remain unresolved. These steps do not establish complete deletion.

To request deletion, contact imperfecttrainer@gmail.com and identify the affected channel. Do not email tokens or passwords. The operator will identify affected current records, historical copies, reports and local copies, pause affected processing, and arrange removal through the applicable account and hosting controls. Completion will not be reported while removal is unverified. A complete procedure covering provider-retained objects and local copies is still being completed; the app remains paused during this work.

Disconnecting Google and managing videos

You can remove the app’s access through your Google account permissions. Revoking access prevents continued use of that connection; it does not itself remove saved GitHub records or videos already on YouTube. Use YouTube Studio to change visibility or delete a YouTube video. Deleting the app’s records does not delete your YouTube content.

This website and provider policies

This website has no sign-in, forms, analytics scripts, advertising, embedded videos or app-set cookies. When hosted on GitHub Pages, GitHub receives technical request information, including visitors’ IP addresses, for security and service operation. Following an external link or sending email involves that provider’s practices.

See the Google Privacy Policy, GitHub Privacy Statement and YouTube Terms of Service.

Changes

The published policy must match the operating tool. Material changes to Google-data access or use require the owner’s informed agreement before that changed use begins.
